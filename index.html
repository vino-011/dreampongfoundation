About Us

Dream Pong Community Sports Foundation is a nonprofit organization dedicated to promoting table tennis, youth development, physical activity, and community engagement.

Charity ID: 42-1844692

Our Mission

Dream Pong Community Sports Foundation is a registered nonprofit organization dedicated to expanding access to table tennis and community sports in Mountain View, California. Our programs include youth table tennis training and camps, after-school sports activities, volunteer and community-service opportunities for high school and college students, corporate team-building events, and free community sports events open to the public.
